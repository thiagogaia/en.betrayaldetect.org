Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what data the Love Vision website and course collect and how it is used. The data controller is LoveVision LDA. Contact: contacto@love-vision.site.

1. Quiz answers

The answers you give in the free check are stored only in your browser for the current session, to build your personalized result. We do not ask for your name, email or phone in the quiz, and the answers are not sent to our servers.

2. Analytics and attribution

We use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity and Utmify to understand how the site is used and which ads bring visitors. These tools may use cookies or similar identifiers and collect information such as device and browser type, approximate location, pages viewed, clicks and scrolling (Clarity may create anonymized session recordings and heatmaps), and campaign parameters from the link you clicked. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

3. Optional public-photo check

If this feature is available and you choose to use it, the phone number you type is sent once to check whether a public profile photo exists, and it is not saved or stored by us. We only record whether a photo was found, never the number.

4. Purchases

Checkout and payment are handled by our payment partner, Monetizze, which processes your name, email and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive the information needed to give you access and support (such as your name, email and order details).

5. Support emails

If you email us, we use your message and email address only to answer you and handle your request.

6. What we don’t do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not access anyone’s phone, accounts or messages.

7. Retention

We keep purchase and support records for as long as needed to provide the course, meet legal and tax obligations and resolve disputes. Analytics data is kept according to each tool’s retention settings.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or restrict certain uses. To make a request, email contacto@love-vision.site. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Age

The site and the course are intended for adults 18 and over.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The version published on this page, with its date, is the one in force.
